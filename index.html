Rural Life Protection Architecture

Safety First Initiative

In rural feeders, undetected line breaks fall across crop fields, irrigation canals, and tractor routes. This system provides proactive alerts to protect villagers, tractors, and livestock from fatal step-potential shock.

Prevents Accidental Contact

Warns farmers to stay away from hazardous zones instead of unknowingly stepping onto energised ground.

Machinery & Irrigation Safety

Protects farmers operating tractors, metal threshers, and irrigation pipe ladders under overhead lines.

Livestock & Cattle Defense

Cattle possess high sensitivity to step voltage. Rapid line isolation (<4s) prevents herd electrocution.

Solar Off-Grid Remote Poles

Autonomous solar-charged sensor nodes on remote towers ensure 24/7 protection without relying on grid power.
